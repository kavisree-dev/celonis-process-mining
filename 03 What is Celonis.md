**What is Celonis?**

**Introduction:**

It is one of the world’s leading companies in Process Mining and Execution Management.

It helps organizations discover, analyze, and improve their business processes using real data from enterprise systems.

Instead of relying only on assumptions, Celonis helps companies understand how work is actually happening inside the business.

It is widely used by global enterprises for process optimization and digital transformation.

---

Who Founded Celonis?

Celonis was founded in Germany by university students who wanted to solve real business inefficiencies using data and technology.

What started as a student startup later became one of the biggest names in Process Mining.

Today, Celonis works with major global companies across industries.

---

**What Does Celonis Do?**

Celonis reads data from systems like:

- SAP
- ERP systems
- CRM platforms
- Oracle
- Salesforce
- HR systems
- Supply chain systems

Using this data, Celonis helps companies:

- identify bottlenecks
- detect delays
- reduce rework
- improve compliance
- optimize process performance
- reduce operational costs
- improve customer experience

It gives organizations full visibility into how their business processes actually work.

---

**Example:**

A company believes its invoice process works like this:

Invoice Received → Approval → Payment

But Celonis may discover:

Invoice Received → Delay → Rework → Approval → Delay → Payment

This helps management understand where time and money are being lost.

---

**What is Execution Management?**

Celonis is not only about finding problems.

It also helps companies take action to fix them.

This is called Execution Management

It means:

- identifying process issues
- understanding root causes
- recommending improvements
- monitoring process performance continuously

This makes Celonis more powerful than simple reporting tools.

---

**Why Celonis Offers Free Courses for Students?**

Celonis provides free learning programs because companies need more skilled professionals in Process Mining.

By helping students learn early, businesses can hire talent that already understands the platform.

This also helps Celonis increase adoption of its technology in the market.

It is both a learning opportunity and a smart business strategy.
