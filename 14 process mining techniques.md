# Process Mining Techniques: Discovery, Conformance, Enhancement

As you've seen, Process Mining turns a data model (i.e. Event Log + Case Table) into an aggregated reconstruction of process flows.

It allows you to obtain data-based insights into the frequency of particular process patterns (or "variants") as well as their impact and dependence on certain Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

In this way, Process Mining ultimately allows you to:
- **Discover** your true as-is process  
- Measure its **conformance** with an ideal model  
- Identify bottlenecks, patterns, and areas of improvement to **enhance** the process and bring it closer to your ideal model  

---

## 1. Discovery

Describes the **direct insights into the as-is process**.

In contrast to traditional Process Management techniques (e.g., human-driven modeling or sampling), Discovery provides objective, data-based insights into the as-is process in its full complexity.

---

## 2. Conformance

Refers to the **comparison of the as-is process to an ideal model**.

Conformance helps determine:
- How far the actual process deviates from the desired model  
- In how many cases these deviations occur  

Additionally, Discovery can help reconstruct a model that is realistic and captures the complexity of the process.

---

## 3. Enhancement

Refers to all actions and improvement measures taken to **bring the as-is process closer to the desired model**.

It includes:
- Feedback from real-world data  
- Predictions  
- Recommendations  

As a research field, Enhancement enables organizations to adapt and use Process Mining effectively, helping generate business value and actionable insights.

---

## To Sum Up: Process Mining at a Glance

1. **Real-World Activities**  
   Real-world activities such as payments, manufacturing, and deliveries take place.

2. **System Support**  
   IT systems supporting these activities capture digital footprints in real time.

3. **Event Logs**  
   Businesses extract these digital footprints via system event logs.

4. **Process Mining Software**  
   Process mining tools aggregate this data and reconstruct processes, allowing you to visualize and analyze what is really happening in your business.
