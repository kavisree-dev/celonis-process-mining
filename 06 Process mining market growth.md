PROCESS MINING MARKET GROWTH:

**Introduction**:

Process mining has become a major business technology used by companies across industries to improve operations, reduce costs, and support digital transformation.

As businesses become more data-driven, the demand for Process Mining continues to grow rapidly.

---

**Fast-Growing Industry**:

In recent years, companies have increased investment in:

- automation
- operational excellence
- process optimization
- enterprise technology
- digital transformation

Because of this, Process Mining has become a high-demand business solution.

Industry reports show strong market growth over the next few years, proving that this field has long-term career potential.

---

**Why Companies Are Investing:**

Modern companies face many challenges such as:

- customer expectations for faster service
- same-day delivery demands
- global supply chain disruptions
- rising operational costs
- talent shortages
- technology disruption
- compliance pressure

To solve these problems, companies need better visibility into how work actually happens.

Process Mining provides that visibility.

This is why organizations are investing heavily in it.

---

**From Research to Real Business**:

Process Mining originally started as an academic research field.

Researchers studied how system event logs could help analyze business processes.

Later, companies like brought this concept into real business operations.

Today, Process Mining is used daily in finance, procurement, HR, supply chain, customer service, and consulting.

It has successfully moved from theory to industry.
