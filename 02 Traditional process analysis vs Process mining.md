TRADITIONAL PROCESS ANALYSIS VS PROCESS MINING:
**Introduction:**

Before Process Mining became popular, companies mainly used traditional methods to understand and improve business processes.

These traditional methods were useful, but they often depended heavily on human assumptions and manual discussions.

Process Mining introduced a more accurate and data-driven approach.

Understanding the difference between both is very important.

---

**Traditional Process Analysis:**

Traditional process analysis usually includes:

- meetings with stakeholders
- employee interviews
- workshops and discussions
- manual flowchart creation
- process documentation based on assumptions

Managers and employees explain how they believe the process works, and based on that information, a process map is created.

This describes the process as it should happen.

---

**Problem with Traditional Analysis:**

The major problem is:

The documented process is often different from the real process.

In reality:

- steps are skipped
- approvals are delayed
- tasks are repeated
- exceptions happen frequently
- employees may follow unofficial shortcuts

Because of this, traditional analysis may not show the full truth.

It reflects opinions, not always reality.

---

**Process Mining Approach:**

Process Mining uses data directly from IT systems.

It analyzes event logs from systems like:

- SAP
- ERP
- CRM
- Oracle
- Salesforce

Instead of asking employees how the process works, Process Mining checks the real execution history from system records.

This shows how the process is actually happening.

---

**Example:**

Expected process:

Purchase Request → Approval → Purchase Order → Payment

Actual process found by Process Mining:

Purchase Request → Delay → Rework → Approval → Additional Approval → Payment

This helps identify hidden inefficiencies.

---

**Key Differences:**

|Traditional Process Analysis| Process Mining                 |
|----------------------------|--------------------------------|
|Based on interviews         | Based on system data           |
|Subjective opinions         | Objective facts                |
|Manual process maps         | Automatically discovered flows |
|Assumptions-based           | Evidence-based                 |
|Periodic reviews            | Continuous monitoring          |

---

**Why Process Mining is Better?**

Process Mining provides:

- better visibility
- faster problem detection
- compliance checking
- root cause analysis
- real-time monitoring
- stronger decision-making

It helps companies reduce guesswork and improve operations with confidence.

---

**Traditional analysis shows how people think the process works.**

**Process Mining shows how the process actually works.**
